Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Clipsy is a clipboard history app for macOS. Keeping what you copy private is the point of the app. In short: Clipsy does not collect, transmit or share any data.

What Clipsy stores

Clipsy keeps a history of the items you copy (text, rich text, images and references to files) so you can paste them again. This history is stored only on your Mac, inside Clipsy's private app container. You can delete single items or clear the whole history at any time, and deleting the app removes it.

What Clipsy sends

Nothing. Clipsy has no network access. There are no accounts, analytics, crash reporters, advertising or tracking of any kind. Text recognition in images is performed entirely on your Mac with Apple's Vision framework.

What Clipsy skips

Items that password managers mark as concealed or transient are never saved. You can also exclude any app from Settings, or pause capturing whenever you like.

Accessibility permission

If you turn on automatic pasting, macOS asks you to allow Clipsy under Accessibility. Clipsy uses this permission only to press ⌘V in the app you were using, right after you choose a clip. It does not read your screen or the contents of other apps. Automatic pasting is optional.

Children

Clipsy does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
